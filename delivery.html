How Hostel Delivery Works

Ordering from PwaniBite is simple. Just follow these steps.

1

Choose Your Meal

Browse our menu and select the food you would like to order.

2

Place Your Order

Enter your name, phone number, hostel, block and room number.

3

We Prepare Your Food

Our canteen team prepares your meal after receiving the order.

4

Food Is Delivered

Our delivery team brings your meal to the hostel and room you provided.
