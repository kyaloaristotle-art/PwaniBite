About PwaniBite

Making food ordering easier for Pwani University comrades.

What is PwaniBite?

PwaniBite is a student-focused food ordering and hostel delivery service designed for comrades at Pwani University.

Our goal is to make it easier for students to enjoy affordable meals without having to leave their hostels every time they feel hungry.

Students can browse the menu, choose their favourite meal, provide their hostel details, and have their order delivered to them.
